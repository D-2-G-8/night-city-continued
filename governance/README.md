# governance/

Project policy. Changes here need an RFC and the maintainer council, not a quick PR.

| File | What it settles |
|---|---|
| `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` | Expected behaviour and how reports are handled |
| `donations.md` | What money may fund, and what it may never buy |
| `sponsorship.md` | Infrastructure sponsorship: allowed, and its hard limits |
| `author-rights.md` | What a mod author is entitled to here. Written for them, not for us |
| `permissions/<mod-id>.md` | Written permission from a mod author — and any later withdrawal — with links and dates |

The rule money cannot bend: nothing is ever behind payment. No early access, no
donor-only builds, no paid priority, no brand placement, no weight in polls or roadmap.

The rule authors can invoke: **an author may have their mod removed at any time, at any tier,
without giving a reason** (ADR-011). No archival copy is kept to keep a release working.
