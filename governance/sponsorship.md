# Sponsorship

> **Status: no sponsors, and none are being sought yet.** This file records the policy that
> has already been decided so there is something to point at when an offer arrives. It sits
> alongside `governance/donations.md`, which covers donations and grants.

## What may be sponsored

**Infrastructure, and only infrastructure:**

- lab hardware — a machine with a graphics card is the project's largest cost,
- hosting and CI capacity,
- a code-signing certificate for the launcher.

A sponsor may provide the thing itself (hardware, credits, runners) or the money for it.

## What a sponsor gets

A mention on the project site and in the monthly financial report. That is the whole list.

## What a sponsor never gets

1. **Nothing in the game.** No brand, no logo, no item, no billboard, no named location, no
   in-game mention of any kind. Placing a real brand inside Cyberpunk 2077 would be
   monetising someone else's IP, it breaks Nexus rules, and undisclosed advertising is
   illegal in the EU, the US and Russia. It would also cost the project the community's
   trust, which is the only thing it actually runs on.
2. **No influence on the roadmap.** Priorities come from fan polls and from the maintainers.
   A sponsor has no vote, no veto, and no seat.
3. **No influence on what is included.** Sponsorship never decides which mods enter the
   distribution, at which tier, or in what order.
4. **No privileges of any kind** — the rules in `governance/donations.md` apply in full. No
   early access, no exclusive builds, no priority support.
5. **No exclusivity.** Sponsoring the project does not buy the right to be the only sponsor,
   or the right to block another.

## How an offer is handled

1. The offer is read against this file. If it carries any condition touching game content,
   the roadmap or inclusion decisions, it is **refused** — the amount is irrelevant.
2. A maintainer decision is recorded; once the council exists, it is the council's call.
3. Accepted sponsorship is **disclosed in the monthly report**: who, what, how much, and what
   it paid for. There is no such thing as a quiet sponsor here.
4. Funds go through the same fiscal host as donations, never through a personal account.

## Ending it

Either side may end sponsorship at any time. Hardware already given stays with the project
unless it was explicitly a loan, and a loan is written down as one when it is accepted.
Ending sponsorship changes nothing about the roadmap, because it never influenced it.
